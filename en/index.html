Organize your photos. Keep the moments you love.

Flipic is a native iPhone photo organizer. For help, contact feedback@returnnull.cc.

Getting started

Swipe horizontally to browse, up to mark for deletion, and down to toggle Photos favorites. Pinch to zoom; return to the fitted size to resume paging. Touch and hold a photo to share it, change its favorite status, or mark it for deletion.

Deletion and undo

Marked photos are hidden from browsing. Tap the trash button to review them, then confirm deletion in the iOS system dialog. Undo restores recent marking and favorite actions. To recover photos already deleted, check Recently Deleted in Photos.

Photo loading

High Quality is the default. PhotoKit may fetch photos from iCloud when local quality is insufficient, which can use cellular data. Choose On Device Only or Original in Settings. Prepare offline copies in advance to keep browsing and organizing in Airplane Mode.

7-day trial and lifetime access

Eligible users can explicitly claim a free 7-day trial, measured from the original verified transaction date. It does not renew or charge automatically. After expiry, browsing, sharing, and the deletion list remain available; swiping down to toggle Favorites and final deletion require lifetime access. A purchase never automatically favorites or deletes photos; act again to confirm.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account originally used for the purchase. Tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or under the membership card in Settings. Restoring an expired trial does not start a new 7-day period. Check your internet connection and App Store account if restoration fails.

Languages

The app supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French, and Spanish. It follows your preferred iPhone languages. You can choose a supported language in the app’s language settings in iOS.

Contact support

Include your app version, iOS version, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue. Hide private photos and personal information before attaching screenshots.
